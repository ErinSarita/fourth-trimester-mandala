A workbook from The Fourth Trimester Mandala

My Fourth Trimester Plan

A place to gather what you will need after your baby arrives: your first hour, your circle of support and what you would love from each of them, your feeding plan, the help that keeps a home running, time to rest, and your birth story when you are ready to tell it. Fill it in with your partner in the last weeks of pregnancy, then share it, so the people around you know how to hold you while you hold your baby.

What you write is saved only in this browser, on this device, and is not sent anywhere. To share it, print it or save it as a PDF. Your birth story is printed only if you choose.

A companion to The Fourth Trimester Mandala, created by Erin Singleton, a student in APPPAH's Prenatal & Perinatal Educator certification. An educational workbook, not medical advice. Share your first hour and feeding plans with your midwife or doctor ahead of time; medical needs come first.
